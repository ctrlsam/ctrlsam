**Platform Engineer by day 🏗️ | Cyber Researcher by night 🛡️**

I spend my daylight hours architecting resilient systems, scaling infrastructure, and optimizing deployment pipelines. When the sun goes down, I pivot into the world of offensive security, hardware hacking, and reverse engineering. 

I am fascinated by systems—not just how to build them to stay up, but how to take them apart, exploit their edge cases, and understand the "why" behind the code. Whether it's transmitting data via lasers, harvesting credentials with high-performance Rust, or automating chess via computer vision, I'm always looking for the next technical rabbit hole.

* **[careless-whisper-python](https://github.com/ctrlsam/careless-whisper-python)**: Security research into **Silent Delivery Receipts** exploits on WhatsApp and Signal.
* **[cred-spider](https://github.com/ctrlsam/cred-spider)**: A high-velocity HTTP credential harvester written in **Rust** with customizable regex rules.

## 👾 Game Research & Automation
* **[chess-bot](https://github.com/ctrlsam/chess-bot)**: An automated chess assistant using **Template Matching** (Computer Vision) to analyze board states on Chess.com and interface with a chess engine.
